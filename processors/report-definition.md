# HAAG weekly reports

Every HAAG researcher submits one report per week. Formats differ per researcher, but every genuine report is the same kind of document: an account of one week of that researcher's own work.

A genuine weekly report accounts for what the researcher did over the week. It lists the things they worked on, and it usually lists blockers too. Many reports have no blockers section at all, and that does NOT make them incomplete.

It usually also carries the researcher's name, a week number, and a date. Length varies widely. A short report is still a report.

Judge by what the document is doing. A weekly report narrates a period of that researcher's own work. Anything that instead describes an object, a dataset or a piece of writing is not a report.

These are NOT weekly reports, even when they carry a researcher's name and a week number:

- a data table, specimen list, figure, chart or appendix that describes an object rather than a week of work
- a paper, draft, literature review or proposal
- meeting notes, agendas, invoices, forms
- a fragment or excerpt of a report rather than the complete document

A document that only describes an artifact is not a report, no matter how it is titled.
